Artistic Activity

Performance and composition as an electroacoustic composer and guitarist, working across computer-based composition and guitar performance.

Forum Wallis 2026 Selection

Current Work

Thresholds of Presence (2025)

An electroacoustic work for guitar and Sherman Filterbank 2, composed in 2025. Three pieces investigate what remains when the computer is removed — the raw signal of a guitar passing through analog filtering, the boundary between the instrument and its transformation, between presence and absence. The title track was selected for Forum Wallis 2026, Leuk Castle, Switzerland.

Max / MSP

Computer & Guitar

Much of the catalog is built on Max/MSP/Jitter, used extensively for multichannel spatialized pieces. Otani authored an introductory book on Max/MSP/Jitter (BNN, 2009) and a technical column series (Sound & Recording Magazine, 1996–1997). Thresholds of Presence is a project-specific departure — built from guitar and Sherman Filterbank 2 alone, without a computer.
