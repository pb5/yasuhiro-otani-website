Artistic Activity

Artistic Activity

Performance and composition as an electroacoustic composer and guitarist. Since 2025, working without a computer — building the ongoing series Thresholds of Presence from guitar and Sherman Filterbank 2 alone.

Guitar + Sherman Filterbank 2 IRCAM Forum Member Forum Wallis 2026 Selection

Current Work

Thresholds of Presence (2025)

An electroacoustic work for guitar and Sherman Filterbank 2, composed in 2025. Three pieces investigate what remains when the computer is removed — the raw signal of a guitar passing through analog filtering, the boundary between the instrument and its transformation, between presence and absence. The title track was selected for Forum Wallis 2026, Leuk Castle, Switzerland.

Max/MSP Background

Before 2025, much of the work made extensive use of Max/MSP/Jitter for multichannel spatialized pieces. Otani authored an introductory book on Max/MSP/Jitter (BNN, 2009) and a technical column series (Sound & Recording Magazine, 1996–1997). Since 2025 the practice has moved to computer-free production.
