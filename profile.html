Bio（英文・プレス向け）

Yasuhiro Otani is a Tokyo-based electroacoustic composer and guitarist whose work explores the boundary between acoustic presence and electronic transformation. Since 2025, his practice has focused exclusively on guitar and Sherman Filterbank 2 — stripping away the computer to investigate the raw threshold between instrument and signal processing.

His recent work Thresholds of Presence (2025) was selected for Forum Wallis 2026, Switzerland. Previous works have been presented at New York Philharmonic Biennial (2016), ARS Electronica / Forum Wallis (2016), ICMC Perth (2013), Asian Composers League (2012, 2013), Re-new Copenhagen (2011), MUSICACOUSTICA Beijing (2010), and major festivals across Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

Otani has been an IRCAM Forum member since 1995 and holds a BA in Computer Science from Tokyo Denki University. He teaches at Showa University of Music and Tokyo Keizai University.
